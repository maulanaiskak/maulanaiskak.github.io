Heat Exchanger

Temperature control system

Overview

The benzene production or hydrodealkylation process begins with combining the toluene feed with a stream of mixed hydrogen and fresh hydrogen gas. Then, the mixture of hydrogen and toluene is preheated using a heater or furnace. In the furnace, the stream is heated to 600 C then introduced into the reactor. The reactor is where the main reaction happens:

C7H8 + H2 → C6H6 + CH4

This reaction is irreversible, and it needs a catalyst. The catalyst consists of chromium or molybdenum oxides, platinum or platinum oxides, on silica or alumina. Another minor reversible side reaction2 is often observed:

2 Benzene = Diphenyl + H2
