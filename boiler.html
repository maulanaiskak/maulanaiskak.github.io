Boiler 31F-28 PT. Badak NGL

Combustion Control System

Overview

A boiler is an operating unit to produce steam with high pressure with water as raw material that has been pre-treated to meet the permitted specifications. The water treatment carried out aims to remove mineral content and impurities in the water. This is because the content of other substances in the water will increase the boiling point of the water itself, which causes the consumption of gas as fuel to be excessive and thus inefficient.

In 2015, an internal energy audit was conducted by Badak LNG. The results of the audit stated that the boiler efficiency at Badak LNG was below the expected efficiency value of 84%. Therefore it is necessary to make improvements in the boiler system. The efficiency of a boiler is influenced by several parameters, such as excess oxygen in flue gas, BFW preheater outlet temperature, and fuel quality.

Based on the previous research conducted by Badak LNG, “Studies for Increasing Boiler Energy Efficiency”, one way that can be done to increase boiler efficiency at Badak LNG is to control excess oxygen in the exhaust gas. The actual excess oxygen value in most boilers often deviates from the design value. Ideally, maximum boiler efficiency will be achieved when the supply of combustion air to the boiler is equal to its stoichiometric requirements for complete combustion to occur where oxygen will completely oxidize the carbon content in the fuel gas. However, an excessive amount of air is required to ensure complete combustion of the boiler.By design, the excess oxygen parameter in the boiler is corrected by a simple bias adjustment by AIC-06. AIC-06 is a control system, which works by reducing excess oxygen so that it is not too much so that boiler performance can be increased. However, the use of AIC-06 still has a weakness, namely, when the bias value is reduced, the desired excess oxygen value is not achieved. This is due to an error in the logic of the combustion control system. Therefore, the author makes a design modification of the logic of the control system and simulates it with Matlab-Simulink software to see if the excess oxygen value in the exhaust gas is as expected.

Existing Control System

The desired design demand is when the excess oxygen set value is reduced to less than the initial excess oxygen value, the air flow rate will decrease but the fuel rate remains constant. With the current logic of the control system, this cannot be fulfilled because when the air flow rate decreases the fuel flow rate will also decrease.

Designed Control System

Modification of the control system logic aims to create a boiler combustion system that is able to keep the value of the rate of fuel entering the boiler constant when the value of the air flow rate is reduced. The modification made is to move the output of the AIC-06 which was initially fed to the FIC-45 set value to be fed to the output of the FT-45 reading. With the transfer of the output from the AIC-06, the Process Value (PV) read is not the actual value of the air flow rate. The PV value received by FIC-45 has been added or subtracted from the value of the output of AIC-06.
